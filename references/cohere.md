# Cohere Design Reference

The local Cohere design analysis informs Gyeol's public site and, more lightly, its document templates.

## Weighting

- Public site: 70 percent influence.
- Document templates: 30 percent influence.

## Site Cues

- White editorial canvas.
- Deep green-black or dark navy bands.
- Large sans display type.
- Thin rules and strong spacing.
- Small, quiet command surfaces.
- Minimal shadows.

## Document Cues

- Calm hierarchy.
- Hairline separators.
- Near-black text with muted slate support.
- Action blue as the main accent.
- Printable density over marketing spaciousness.
