Investor Memo: Haneul NPU Systems

Haneul NPU Systems

Executive Summary

Haneul NPU Systems is presented with fictional sample data to test a premium investor memo for an on-device NPU company.

Why Now

AI workloads are moving toward hybrid execution, and device makers need lower power inference close to the user.

Product Surface

A low-power inference chiplet, reusable NPU IP, compiler support, and OEM integration kits.

Market Wedge

Start with OEMs that need private, low-latency AI features without sending sensitive user data to cloud inference.

Business Model

Licensing, NRE, and chiplet revenue tied to pilot OEM ramps and reference designs.

Technology Moat

The core moat is a low-power inference chiplet and reusable NPU IP platform. validated against 38 TOPS prototype targets.

Risk Register

Foundry access, OEM concentration, benchmark credibility, and competition from in-house silicon remain the main risks.

Investment View

The opportunity is attractive if pilots convert into durable design wins before larger incumbents compress the wedge.
